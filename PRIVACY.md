# Privacy Policy

**YouTube Presence for Discord** — last updated 2026-08-27

This extension does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information, and it
does not send any data to the developer or to any third party.

## What it reads

While you are on YouTube, the extension reads the currently playing video's title,
channel, timestamps, and thumbnail URL from the page's player.

## Where that goes

Only to a companion desktop app running on **your own computer** (`http://127.0.0.1`),
which displays it as your Discord status. Nothing is sent to any external server, to the
developer, or to any third party. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking.

## Permissions

- Access to `http://127.0.0.1/*` is used solely to talk to the local desktop app.
- The extension runs only on YouTube pages (`youtube.com` and `music.youtube.com`).
