You're almost set up

Thanks for installing! This extension reads what you're playing on YouTube, but it needs one small free desktop app to actually put it on your Discord profile. Two quick steps:

1

Get the desktop app and run it

Download it, open it once, and a small red play icon appears in your system tray (near the clock). Right-click it to start it with Windows if you want.

Download the app
2

Play something on YouTube

That's it. Your Discord status updates on its own while a video or song is playing - on youtube.com and on YouTube Music.

Private: nothing leaves your computer. The extension only talks to your own PC (127.0.0.1) - no accounts, no tracking.

Heads up: the "Listen Along" and "Play on YouTube" buttons show to other people, not on your own profile - same as Spotify.
